### 3. Visual Identity Differentiators

1. **Card Frame Architecture:**
   - 5:7 aspect ratio.
   - Arched stained-glass spirit art window.
   - Ornamental corner filigree.
   - Aspect gradient outer rim (Ember, Tide, Verdant, Volt, Umbra, Lumen, Iron, Aether).
   - Bottom center number plate.
   - Never yellow borders, never 'ex' tags.

2. **Procedural Character Art Generation:**
   - Every card is bound to an original character illustration generated by `characterEngine.ts` from the card id + name (10 silhouette classes, 5 combat poses, per-card proportions, accessories, aspect palettes, and scene backdrops).
   - Static SVGs for all 100 cards ship in `frontend/public/cards/art/c-XXX.svg` and are regenerated deterministically by `scripts/generate-character-art.mjs`.
   - Battle states (attack / hurt / knockout) are driven from global CSS over the inline SVG; idle breathing, aura, and weapon shimmer live inside the SVG itself.

3. **Mascot & Symbolism:**
   - Original sacred **Wisp-Moth** mascot on concentric rings.
   - Eight original Aspect glyphs (flame mote, wave crest, sprout, forked glint, crescent shadow, sunburst eye, hex bolt, spiral dot).
